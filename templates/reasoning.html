How CityCare derives an answer

Facts are stored in JSON; pyDatalog evaluates formal rules. Derived predicates are never saved as facts.

Core concepts

Predicate
A relationship or property, such as specializes.
Constant
A known value, such as kumar or orthopedics.
Variable
An uppercase placeholder, such as Doctor.
Fact
Stored knowledge, such as specializes(kumar, orthopedics).
